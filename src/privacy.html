Privacy Policy

Our commitment to privacy

auuki.com ("us", "we", or "our") operates the auuki.com website (hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

Auuki is local-only by default. Your workouts, recordings and coaching data stay on your device unless you explicitly enable an integration or server account. The optional self-hosted server account is separate from third-party integration accounts. Enabling server coaching stores your coaching profile, goals, activity history, derived training state, plans and feedback on the host's server. Saving a device backup additionally uploads imported workouts, recent FIT recordings, profile settings and your manual calendar. Do not enable these features unless you trust the server operator.

What information we collect

Your email is collected during the optional registration process.

The self-hosted backend also stores a salted password hash and session records. Server coaching processes physiological training data and optional subjective feedback to provide deterministic training recommendations, not medical advice. No external AI provider is used. Server operators must establish appropriate consent, security and retention practices for their users. We do not rent, buy or sell personal data.

Mobile device access

If you use our application(s), we may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, namely bluetooth. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to preserve your logged-in state across page refreshes on auuki.com.

Data storage

With a self-hosted server account, data is stored wherever that server's operator hosts it. Contact that operator for the applicable location and retention policy. Local-only use does not upload data to the optional backend. Third-party integrations have their own storage and privacy policies.

Data retention

You can download a server backup, export coaching history, and delete your self-hosted server account from the app. Account deletion removes live server documents and sessions but does not erase local device data or operator backups; the operator is responsible for backup retention. Signing out alone does not delete server data.

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. No purpose in this notice will require us keeping your personal information for longer than the period of time in which users have an account with us.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will delete such information, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your personal information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click a third party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.
