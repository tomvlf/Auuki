Privacy Policy

Our commitment to privacy

athlima.com ("us", "we", or "our") operates the athlima.com website (hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

Athlima runs in your browser without login or an application backend. Your workouts, recordings, coaching profile, goals, activity history, plans and feedback are stored on this device. Local backups are downloaded files, not uploads to an Athlima server.

What information we collect

No email, password or account registration is required. Coaching processes training data and optional feedback locally to provide deterministic training recommendations, not medical advice. No external AI provider is used.

If you connect Intervals.icu, its personal API key is stored in your browser's local storage and sent directly to Intervals.icu over HTTPS. Imports retrieve provider data into local history; uploads send selected FIT recordings to Intervals.icu. The key is not included in local backups. Anyone with access to this browser profile can access the key and local training data.

Mobile device access

If you use our application(s), we may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, namely bluetooth. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings.

How we use cookies

The application does not use login or session cookies. Browser storage preserves local settings and training data.

Data storage

Training data is stored in this browser profile for this site's origin. Optional Intervals.icu integration is governed by the provider's own storage and privacy policies. A static hosting operator may keep ordinary web access logs but receives no application training-state uploads.

Data retention

You can export coaching history and download a local backup from Settings. Clear this site's browser data to remove local data and credentials. Disconnecting Intervals.icu removes its local key but keeps imported history; revoke the key at Intervals.icu if needed. Browser eviction can also remove data, so keep backups. Local deletion does not remove downloaded files, provider copies, or data retained from older server-backed versions.

Whom we share your personal data with

IT support, when necessary Public authorities: when we are obliged to report to them We require that all such recipients secure data in accordance with good information security and as per the requirements of this Privacy notice.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

Links to Other Sites

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click a third party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.
